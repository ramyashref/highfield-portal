      Certification Requirements

      Assessment & Certification

      To qualify for the program certificate, participants must fulfill ALL of the following requirements:

      1. Attend both training days in full.
      2. Successfully pass the theoretical examination.
      3. Successfully pass the practical examination.
      Important Notice

      Attendance alone does not guarantee certification. Participants must attend both training days in full and successfully pass both the theoretical and practical examinations. Participants who fail to meet any of these requirements will not be eligible to receive the certificate.

      Certificate: Basic Life Support (BLS) – Egyptian Health Council accreditation.
