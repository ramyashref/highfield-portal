About SERAG

Advancing Women's Intimate Health

Pre-Congress Workshops

Five focused workshops designed to connect clinical assessment, procedural skills and evidence-informed decision-making. Dates are currently listed as TBA.

6 hours9 AM – 3 PMDate: TBA

Vulvar Variability and Comprehensive Systematic Aesthetic-Functional Examination of the External Genitalia Using the Three-Rings Model

A practical, examination-focused workshop introducing the spectrum of normal vulvar variation. Participants learn a structured approach to assessment through the Three-Rings Model and documentation that informs management planning.

Workshop objectives

  • Recognize the range of normal vulvar variability.
  • Use objective scales to assess cosmetic perception.
  • Perform a systematic aesthetic-functional examination using the Three-Rings Model.
  • Measure the dimensions of the vulva, vagina and perineum.
  • Document findings in a clear scientific format.
ModeratorMostafa Seleem
6 hours9 AM – 3 PMDate: TBA

Hyaluronic Acid Injection for Vaginal Dryness, Vestibulodynia, G-Spot Augmentation, and Labial Reshaping

A workshop on hyaluronic acid fillers in female intimate regions, covering product selection, site-specific injection techniques and patient selection, with emphasis on safety and management of adverse events.

Workshop objectives

  • Identify types of hyaluronic acid used in the female intimate area.
  • Understand techniques appropriate to each product and anatomical site.
  • Apply techniques to the listed clinical indications.
  • Recognise, prevent and manage potential side effects and complications.
ModeratorMostafa Seleem
6 hours9 AM – 3 PMDate: TBA

PRP and PRF: Preparation and Injection Techniques for Gynecological Conditions

A practical workshop on platelet-rich plasma (PRP) and platelet-rich fibrin (PRF), including preparation, differences between products and selection of injection technique and volume for different gynecological indications.

Workshop objectives

  • Select the most suitable PRP kit for a given indication.
  • Distinguish between PRP and PRF and prepare each correctly.
  • Understand injection techniques and the volume required for each indication.
ModeratorMostafa Seleem
6 hours9 AM – 3 PMDate: TBA

The Six-Domain Clinical Assessment Model of Female Orgasmic Function

A clinical workshop presenting a structured six-domain model for evaluating female orgasmic function, combining anatomy, history, examination and objective tools to build an individualized multidisciplinary management plan.

Workshop objectives

  • Describe the anatomy underlying female orgasmic function.
  • Understand the six-domain clinical assessment model.
  • Use objective tools to assess female sexual dysfunction.
  • Apply clinical data to multidisciplinary management planning.
ModeratorMostafa Seleem
6 hours9 AM – 3 PMDate: TBA

Your Introduction to ICSI

A foundation workshop for clinicians new to intracytoplasmic sperm injection (ICSI), following the treatment pathway from patient selection and ovarian stimulation through monitoring, oocyte retrieval, embryo transfer and patient counselling.

Workshop objectives

  • Identify the indications for ICSI and factors influencing outcomes.
  • Recognise ovulation induction protocols and selection principles.
  • Monitor follicular maturation and endometrial preparation.
  • Understand oocyte pick-up and embryo transfer techniques.
  • Plan endometrial preparation for frozen embryo transfer and counsel patients.
ModeratorMostafa Seleem
