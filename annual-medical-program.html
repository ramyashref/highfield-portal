ABOUT THE PROGRAM

HIGHFIELD SCHOLARS is a comprehensive annual package that brings together all 33 training programs scheduled across 12 months. Enrollment is for the complete package and cannot be split into individual monthly subscriptions or standalone courses. The program includes 75 planned training days. Certificates are issued according to the requirements of each program.
