Eligibility Requirements

Who Can Apply?

  • A Doctorate degree from a recognised Egyptian university.
  • Or a Master's degree with an accredited Training of Trainers (TOT) certificate.
  • Relevant academic, clinical, professional, or technical experience in the applicant's specialty.
  • Previous teaching or professional training experience is preferred.
  • Accreditation by the Egyptian Health Council is preferred and will strengthen the application.
Important: Egyptian Health Council trainer accreditation is preferred, but it is not a mandatory condition for submitting the application.
Required Information

Application Details

  • Professional field and primary area of expertise.
  • Professional licence number.
  • Faculty and university awarding the academic degree.
  • Current workplace and professional position.
  • Years of professional and training experience.
  • An updated Curriculum Vitae.
A National ID copy and professional licence card copy are optional at this stage and are not required to submit the initial application.
