Eligibility

Trainer Requirements

Applicants must meet the relevant academic and professional requirements before submitting their documents for review.

  • Hold a Doctorate degree from an Egyptian university.
  • Or hold a Master's degree and have successfully completed a Training of Trainers (TOT) course from an accredited organization.
  • Be accredited by the Egyptian Health Council as a trainer, where required for the proposed training activity.
  • Possess relevant professional experience in the proposed field of training.
  • Previous teaching or professional training experience is preferred.
Selection Process

Application Review

Applications are reviewed according to academic qualifications, professional experience, training competence, submitted documentation, and the current needs of Highfield Egypt's programs.

  • Initial review of the submitted qualifications and documents.
  • Academic and professional profile assessment.
  • Interview or presentation assessment when required.
  • Final selection according to available training opportunities.
  • Submitting an application does not guarantee acceptance.
Required Documents

Prepare Your Application

Please prepare clear and valid copies of the following documents before starting the application process.

🪪

National ID

A clear copy of the applicant's valid Egyptian national ID.

🩺

Professional License

A valid professional practice licence or professional syndicate card.

📄

Updated CV

An updated curriculum vitae detailing academic and professional experience.

🎓

Doctorate Certificate

A clear copy of the Doctorate degree certificate issued by an Egyptian university.
