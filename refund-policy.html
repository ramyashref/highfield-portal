Refund & Cancellation Policy

Clear, transparent, and fair guidelines for registration cancellations and refunds.

Last Updated: November 2026
At Highfield Egypt, we are committed to providing high-quality educational and professional development services. This policy explains the terms and conditions governing registration cancellations, refund requests, and payments for our training programs, workshops, conferences, and educational services.

01 General Policy

This Refund & Cancellation Policy applies to all participants registering for educational programs, professional training courses, workshops, conferences, and other services provided by Highfield Egypt.

We aim to ensure transparency and fairness in all registration and payment procedures.

02 Participant Cancellation Rights

Participants have the right to cancel their registration up to 48 hours before the scheduled start of the training program, workshop, or event.

To request cancellation, participants must contact the Highfield Egypt team through the official communication channels.

Cancellation Deadline 48 Hours

Cancellation requests must be submitted at least 48 hours before the scheduled starting time.

03 Refund Policy

Participants who submit their cancellation requests at least 48 hours before the scheduled start of the program are entitled to:

100% Refund Full refund with ZERO deductions.

No administrative fees, cancellation charges, or additional deductions will be applied.

The refund will be processed within 10 business days from the date the cancellation request is approved.

04 Cancellation Requests Within 48 Hours

Cancellation requests submitted less than 48 hours before the scheduled start of a program may not qualify for a refund under the standard cancellation policy.

Such requests will be reviewed individually, taking into consideration the circumstances and applicable consumer protection regulations.

05 Cancellation or Changes by Highfield Egypt

If Highfield Egypt cancels a training program, workshop, or conference, registered participants will be notified through their registered contact details.

Participants will be entitled to a 100% refund of the amount paid for the cancelled service, without any deductions, within 10 business days.

If a program is rescheduled or substantially modified, participants will be informed and offered appropriate arrangements in accordance with applicable regulations and consumer rights.

06 Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be returned through the original payment method whenever possible.

  • Refund processing takes up to 10 business days from the date of approval.
  • No deductions will be applied to eligible refunds.
  • The time required for the amount to appear in the participant's account may depend on the payment provider or banking institution.

07 Non-Attendance

Failure to attend a scheduled training session, workshop, or conference without submitting a cancellation request within the applicable cancellation period does not automatically qualify the participant for a refund.

Exceptions may be considered on a case-by-case basis and in accordance with applicable laws.

08 Registration Transfer

Participants may request to transfer their registration to another individual or to a future batch of the same program.

All transfer requests are subject to prior approval by Highfield Egypt and the availability of seats.

09 Legal Compliance

This policy is governed by the applicable laws and regulations of the Arab Republic of Egypt, including Consumer Protection Law No. 181 of 2018 and its Executive Regulations.

Nothing in this policy is intended to limit or waive any mandatory rights granted to consumers under applicable Egyptian law.

In the event of any conflict between this policy and applicable legislation, the applicable legal provisions shall prevail.
