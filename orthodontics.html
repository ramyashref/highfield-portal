About the Program

Build confidence from diagnosis to treatment planning.

The Basic & Advanced Orthodontic Professional Program brings together the fundamentals of orthodontic diagnosis and clinical records with biomechanics, space management, appliance techniques and advanced case discussions. Teaching combines scientific lectures, practical demonstrations and clinical training, as specified in the program agenda.

Scientific Curriculum

Explore the program content

Expand each module to review the key topics in the supplied program agenda.

01Orthodontic FundamentalsDiagnosis, records and classification+
  • Introduction to Orthodontics
  • Classification and etiology of malocclusion
  • Orthodontic diagnosis and orthodontic file
  • Photographs and case presentation
  • Cephalometric tracing and analysis
02Biomechanics & Tooth MovementForce systems, anchorage and clinical mechanics+
  • Orthodontic armamentarium and materials
  • Orthodontic biomechanics and tooth movement
  • Space management, crowding and space creation
  • Anchorage and loss of anchorage
  • Single-step and two-step retraction
03Expansion & Crossbite ManagementTransverse discrepancies and appliances+
  • RME, MARPE and SARPE
  • Scissor bite and crossbite management
  • Hyrax demonstrations and protocols
  • Space analysis and case club discussions
04Appliances & BondingFixed, removable and digital approaches+
  • Digital orthodontics
  • Aligners and removable appliances
  • Direct and indirect bracket bonding
  • Bonding to ceramic crowns
  • Bracket and button bonding on models and in clinic
  • Derotation and lacing techniques
05Anchorage & MiniscrewsTADs and advanced anchorage applications+
  • Miniscrews in orthodontics
  • Insertion practice on models
  • Miniscrew-assisted anchorage concepts
  • Clinical applications in complex tooth movement
06Advanced Cases & FinishingOrthopedics, impaction and retention+
  • Orthopedic treatment and skeletal discrepancies
  • Camouflage case discussions
  • Impaction mechanics and CBCT interpretation
  • Wire-bending and finishing mechanics
  • Retention protocols and retreatment concepts
