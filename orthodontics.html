THE PROGRAM

Build confidence in
orthodontic practice.

The Basic and Advanced Orthodontic Professional Program brings together core orthodontic concepts, treatment planning, case discussions and practical clinical sessions across six weeks.

The training schedule includes theoretical lectures and practical workshops, including cephalometric analysis, orthodontic mechanics, aligners, miniscrews, orthopedic appliances, finishing and wire-bending techniques.
