THE PROGRAM

Build confidence in
orthodontic practice.

The Basic and Advanced Orthodontic Professional Program brings together core orthodontic concepts, treatment planning, case discussions and practical clinical sessions across six weeks.

Topics in the supplied materials include cephalometric analysis, orthodontic biomechanics, aligners, miniscrews, orthopedic and interceptive appliances, camouflage treatment, case-based learning and wire-bending techniques.

01Basic & Advanced OrthodonticsDiagnosis · Treatment planning · Clinical practice

Lectures

  • Introduction and classification of malocclusion.
  • Orthodontic diagnosis and etiology of malocclusion.
  • Cephalometric analysis.
  • Orthodontic armamentarium, biomechanics and tooth movement.
  • Space management, crowding and space creation.
  • RME, MARPE and SARPE; scissor bite and crossbite management.
  • Digital orthodontics, aligners and removable appliances.
  • Direct and indirect bracket bonding, bonding to ceramic crowns, anchorage and loss of anchorage.
  • Single-step and two-step retraction; management of Class I malocclusion, impacted teeth and vertical discrepancies.

Workshops & practical sessions

  • Clinical photography, case presentation, cephalometric tracing and analysis.
  • Orthodontic instruments and materials demonstrations; space analysis.
  • Hyrax demonstration and Hyrax/MARPE protocol.
  • Bracket and button bonding on models and patients; removable appliance demonstrations.
  • Derotation using lacing technique, case clubs and digital scanning.
02Ortho-Pedo DayOrthopedic & interceptive treatment in mixed dentition

Includes skeletal and nonskeletal problems in mixed dentition, with lectures, workshops and clinical training as described in the supplied Ortho-Pedo file.

Skeletal problems

  • Skeletal Class II: mandibular deficiency (Twin Block), maxillary excess (high-pull headgear), and combined approaches.
  • Skeletal Class III: maxillary deficiency (maxillary splint and face mask) and mandibular excess (chin cap).
  • Maxillary constriction, including Hyrax and bonded Hyrax approaches.
  • Vertical maxillary excess and gummy-smile appliances.
  • Cleft lip and palate appliance approaches, and hybrid appliance technique.

Nonskeletal problems

  • Anterior crossbite and 4×2 appliances; posterior crossbite; bad oral habits.
  • Functional mandibular shift, pseudo Class III and facial asymmetry.
  • Ectopic eruption, early dental crowding and altered passive eruption.
  • Supernumerary teeth, premature loss or retention of deciduous teeth, congenitally missing teeth, serial extraction and diastema.

Workshops & clinic

  • Cephalometric analysis and maturity-indicator analysis.
  • Application of orthopedic and removable appliances on models; palatal miniscrews on resin models.
  • Mixed-dentition arch-length analysis and 4×2 appliance bonding.
  • Case discussions for supernumerary teeth and traction of impacted central incisors and canines.
  • Clinical appliance procedures listed in the supplied file, including Twin Block, Hyrax, face-mask and habit-breaking appliances.
03Wire Bending DayArch wires · Loops · Finishing bends

Lectures

  • Leveling and alignment arches.
  • Anterior and posterior space-closure arches.
  • Arch sequences for Class I treatment with mild/moderate crowding, severe crowding, bimaxillary proclination and multiple spaces.
  • Arch sequences for traction of impacted canines and gummy-smile correction.
  • Clinical tips for orthodontic archwires, including SWA and self-ligating technique.

Workshop

  • T-loop archwires for anterior/posterior space closure and incisor intrusion/retraction.
  • Protraction arch for Class III camouflage; Bachman power set; individual torque.
  • Multiloop, big-step and Ballista archwires for camouflage or impacted-canine traction.
  • Sweep arch, Spee arch, CIA intrusive arch, Ricketts arch and uprighting springs.
  • Modified MEWA archwire, molar-uprighting shoe loop, tie-back loop, sliding jigs and sectional T-loop/delta-box loop.
  • First-, second- and third-order finishing bends.
04TADs & Clinical Miniscrew InsertionAnchorage planning · Insertion sites · Clinical applications

Lectures

  • TAD principles, insertion sites, design planning and insertion techniques.
  • Biomechanics of TADs and correction of skeletal Class II and Class III cases.
  • Molar distalization, intrusion and protraction; en-masse retraction of six anterior teeth.
  • Scissor-bite correction, clinical tips, failure and complications.

Workshop

  • Maxillary buccal interradicular, zygomatic, IZC and BSS miniscrews.
  • Mandibular buccal interradicular and midline/maxillary palatal miniscrews.
  • MARPE miniscrews, gummy-smile TADs and ramus TADs.

Clinical day

  • Treatment of orthodontic patients.
  • Clinical insertion of miniscrews.
05Case Club DayCase-based learning · Treatment planning · Complex cases

Lectures

The supplied file describes 50 orthodontic cases, including:

  • Orthopedic cases for skeletal Class II and Class III; interceptive cases for nonskeletal mixed-dentition problems.
  • Class II and Class III camouflage, including skeletal gummy smile.
  • Severe and moderate crowding, complex and multidisciplinary cases.
  • Cleft lip and palate, orthognathic surgery, impacted canines and central incisors.
  • Retreatment of failed orthodontic cases.

Workshop

  • Modified Class III elastics (BSS) and interradicular TADs.
  • Sliding jig, gummy-smile mechanics, gingival and occlusal big step.
  • Sectional T-loop and delta-box loop, sweep arch, CIA arch, Yen-Yang arch, Ballista loop and IZC miniscrew.
06Camouflage DaySkeletal Class II · Skeletal Class III · Gummy smile

Lectures

  • Diagnosis and treatment planning for skeletal Class II, skeletal Class III and gummy smile.
  • Class III correction with Alexander mechanics; self-ligating mechanics and extraction-based camouflage.
  • Class III camouflage with high mandibular-plane angle, congenitally missing upper lateral incisors, TMJ disorder or lower-incisor extraction.
  • Class II camouflage through distalization, severe anterior crowding, upper first-premolar extraction, Hyrax and MARPE.
  • Class II camouflage in short-face and long-face patterns.
  • Gummy-smile mechanics using miniscrews or intrusive arches.

Workshop

  • Modified Class III elastics (BSS) and interradicular miniscrews.
  • Multiloop arch in Alexander Class III mechanics and TMJ splints during camouflage.
  • Protraction arch, sliding jig, TADs for upper distalization and MARPE.
  • TADs for en-masse retraction of upper anteriors, gummy-smile mechanics and incisor intrusion.
  • Intrusive arches: sweep arch, Ricketts arch, CIA arch and reverse curve of Spee.
07Orthodontic BiomechanicsForce systems · Anchorage · Tooth movement mechanics

Lectures

  • Introduction to force, moment of force and moment of couple.
  • Torque: real, relative, segmented, individual and torque control.
  • En-masse retraction of four and six anterior teeth.
  • Molar distalization, protraction and uprighting; molar arm, power arm and lever arm.
  • Maximum and absolute anchorage; incisor intrusion and gummy-smile mechanics.
  • Open-bite, crossbite and scissor-bite correction.
  • Sectional mechanics for canine retraction; ectopic canines and central incisors.
  • Roller-coaster phenomenon and bracket positioning considerations.

Workshop

  • First-, second- and third-order bends; segmented upper-incisor and posterior-tooth torque.
  • Gable (V) bend, sliding jig, molar arm, power arm, lever arm and 3D lever arm.
  • Interradicular maxillary buccal and palatal miniscrews, gummy-smile miniscrews, zygomatic and IZC miniscrews, MARPE miniscrews, BSS miniscrew.
  • Christe tie, T-loop sectional canine retraction, piggyback mechanics, delta-box sectional canine retraction and 16 stainless-steel disimpaction loop.

Curriculum wording is summarized from the seven supplied PDFs. Topics are grouped for website readability; no separate dates are assigned to the specialist-day modules.
