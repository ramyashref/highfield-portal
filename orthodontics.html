Program Curriculum Overview

A structured 6-week program covering foundational concepts, clinical applications, and advanced orthodontic techniques.

01

Week 1Diagnosis & Records

  • Introduction & classification
  • Orthodontic diagnosis
  • Etiology of malocclusion
  • Cephalometric analysis
02

Week 2Clinical Records & Analysis

  • Photographs & case presentation
  • Cephalometric tracing (hands-on)
  • Clinical record analysis
  • Case discussion
03

Week 3Biomechanics & Expansion

  • Orthodontic armamentarium
  • Biomechanics & tooth movement
  • Space management
  • RME / MARPE / SARPE
  • Crossbite management
04

Week 4Instruments & Clinical Skills

  • Instrument & materials demo
  • Space analysis (hands-on)
  • Hyrax demo & protocol
  • Case club
05

Week 5Bonding & Miniscrews

  • Digital orthodontics
  • Brackets bonding (direct & indirect)
  • Miniscrews in orthodontics
  • Removable appliances (hands-on)
06

Week 6Advanced Clinical Topics

  • Hybrid appliances
  • Scanning for digital orthodontics
  • Case management (Class II / III)
  • Camouflage cases
  • Finishing & retention

Certificate & Professional Development Points

Upon fulfilling the program requirements and successfully completing the program, trainees receive a certificate from Princess Fatima Academy for Professional Medical Education – Ministry of Health and Population. The program is accredited by the Egyptian Health Council for 20 Continuing Professional Development (CPD) points, which may be used toward professional license renewal, subject to applicable requirements.
