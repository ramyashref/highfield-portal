About the Program

Build confidence from diagnosis to treatment planning.

The Basic & Advanced Orthodontic Professional Program brings together the fundamentals of orthodontic diagnosis and clinical records with biomechanics, space management, appliance techniques and advanced case discussions. Teaching combines scientific lectures, practical demonstrations and clinical training, as specified in the program agenda.

Weekly Highlights

Six weeks of focused orthodontic learning

Illustrated topic highlights to help trainees preview the main learning themes. The full day-by-day timetable is available below as a downloadable PDF.

Week 01

Diagnosis & Records

Malocclusion classification, orthodontic diagnosis, photography and cephalometric analysis.

Week 02

Appliances & Biomechanics

Instruments, materials, tooth movement, space management and appliance demonstrations.

Week 03

Bonding & Miniscrews

Bonding techniques, anchorage, miniscrew principles and practical insertion training.

Week 04

Case Planning & Camouflage

Malocclusion management, skeletal cases, impacted teeth and case-based workshops.

Week 05

Clinical Appliances & Case Review

Clinical appliance setups, treatment demonstrations and structured case discussions.

Week 06

Wire Bending, Finishing & Retention

Wire bends, elastics, torque, finishing challenges, retention and retreatment concepts.

  • Digital orthodontics
  • Aligners and removable appliances
  • Direct and indirect bracket bonding

Certificate & Professional Development Points

Upon fulfilling the program requirements and successfully completing the program, trainees receive a certificate from Princess Fatima Academy for Professional Medical Education – Ministry of Health and Population. The program is accredited by the Egyptian Health Council for 20 Continuing Professional Development (CPD) points, which may be used toward professional license renewal, subject to applicable requirements.
