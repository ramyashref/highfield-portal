A four-stage progression from diagnosis to confident clinical decision-making.

Program Curriculum Overview

A structured 6-week program covering foundational concepts, clinical applications, and advanced orthodontic techniques.

01

Week 1Diagnosis & Records

  • Introduction & classification
  • Orthodontic diagnosis
  • Etiology of malocclusion
  • Cephalometric analysis
02

Week 2Clinical Records & Analysis

  • Photographs & case presentation
  • Cephalometric tracing (hands-on)
  • Clinical record analysis
  • Case discussion
03

Week 3Biomechanics & Expansion

  • Orthodontic armamentarium
  • Biomechanics & tooth movement
  • Space management
  • RME / MARPE / SARPE
  • Crossbite management
04

Week 4Instruments & Clinical Skills

  • Instrument & materials demo
  • Space analysis (hands-on)
  • Hyrax demo & protocol
  • Case club
05

Week 5Bonding & Miniscrews

  • Digital orthodontics
  • Brackets bonding (direct & indirect)
  • Miniscrews in orthodontics
  • Removable appliances (hands-on)
06

Week 6Advanced Clinical Topics

  • Hybrid appliances
  • Scanning for digital orthodontics
  • Case management (Class II / III)
  • Camouflage cases
  • Finishing & retention

Certification

Upon successfully completing the program and fulfilling all program requirements, trainees will receive a certificate from Princess Fatima Academy for Professional Medical Education – Ministry of Health and Population, bearing the Republic’s emblem and accredited by the Egyptian Health Council, with 20 Continuing Professional Development (CPD) points.

✦

Accreditation

Egyptian Health Council accreditation with 20 CPD points, subject to applicable requirements. These points may be used toward professional license renewal in accordance with the applicable rules.
