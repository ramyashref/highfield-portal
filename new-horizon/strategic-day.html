NEW HORIZON · INTERNATIONAL PEDIATRIC SURGERY CONFERENCE

A wider vision for the future of care.

15 January 2027 · Grand Egyptian Museum, Giza. A high-level gathering to translate scientific outcomes into institutional collaboration and future initiatives.

The day’s proposed activities include:

01 / OPENING

VIP Reception & Official Opening

Welcome official guests and deliver remarks from the organising and partner entities.

02 / OUTCOMES

Scientific Recommendations

Present a concise overview of the outcomes and recommendations from the scientific conference days.

03 / STRATEGY

Future of Pediatric Surgery

Hold a strategic session on the future of pediatric surgery and the development of surgical services.

04 / LEADERSHIP

Dialogue with Leaders & Experts

Facilitate a leadership discussion and explore cooperation among universities, hospitals, and training institutions.

05 / PARTNERSHIPS

Future Initiatives & Partnerships

Discuss potential initiatives and institutional partnerships, subject to approval.

06 / RECOGNITION

Recognition & Strategic Networking

Recognise scientific contributors, partners, and sponsors, with opportunities for strategic meetings and professional networking. A formal dinner may be considered subject to budget and venue arrangements.

Planning note

The activities above reflect the organiser’s proposal. Detailed timings, speakers, participation criteria, and final venue arrangements are subject to confirmation.
