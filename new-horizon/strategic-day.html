NEW HORIZON · INTERNATIONAL PEDIATRIC SURGERY CONFERENCE

A wider vision for the future of care.

A proposed strategic gathering at the Grand Egyptian Museum, bringing leadership, institutions, and the pediatric surgery community into one conversation.

The Strategic Day is intended to create space for high-level dialogue around the future of pediatric surgical care, institutional collaboration, workforce development, innovation, and sustainable improvements in children’s health. Venue access, date, program, and participation arrangements must be confirmed officially before publication.

01 / LEADERSHIP

Strategic Dialogue

Bring clinical leaders and decision-makers together to identify shared priorities and opportunities.

02 / INSTITUTIONS

Partnership & Collaboration

Explore practical ways for institutions, professional bodies, and industry to work together.

03 / THE FUTURE

Innovation & Impact

Discuss education, technology, and systems that can improve outcomes for children.

Strategic agenda to be announced
