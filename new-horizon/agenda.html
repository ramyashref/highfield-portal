DAY 02 · STRATEGIC & LEADERSHIP DAY

From Scientific Insight to Institutional Impact

15 January 2027 · Grand Egyptian Museum, Giza

15 January 2027

A high-level gathering for medical leaders, university and department heads, senior pediatric surgeons, representatives of healthcare and academic institutions, partners, and sponsors. The day is designed to translate the conference’s scientific outcomes into opportunities for collaboration and institutional development.

01 / OPENING

Official Welcome & Conference Outcomes

Reception of VIPs and official guests, opening remarks from the organising and partner entities, and a concise presentation of key recommendations from the scientific conference.

02 / STRATEGIC DIALOGUE

The Future of Pediatric Surgery

A strategic discussion on priorities for developing pediatric surgical services, bringing together medical leaders and experts.

03 / COLLABORATION

Institutional Partnerships & Future Initiatives

Explore opportunities for cooperation among universities, hospitals, and training institutions, and discuss future initiatives and partnerships subject to approval.

04 / RECOGNITION

Recognition & Professional Networking

Recognise scientific contributors, partners, and sponsors, with time for strategic meetings and professional networking.

Programme note

This is the strategic-day framework described in the organiser’s proposal. Detailed timings, speakers, official participation, and venue arrangements remain subject to final confirmation.
